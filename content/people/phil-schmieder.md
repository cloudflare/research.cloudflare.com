Phil Schmieder is a PhD student in cryptography at the University of Wuppertal, Germany. He received his M. Sc. in Computer Science in 2024 and completed his B. Sc. in Applied Mathematics alonside an appreticeship as a Mathematical-Technical Software Engineer in 2018 at the Jülich Supercomputing Centre. His research interests include post-quantum secure cryptography and secure multi-party computation. His personal website is available at [phil-schmieder.de](phil-schmieder.de).

From June 2026 to September 2026, he was a Research Intern at Cloudflare, where he worked on the post-quantum transition of access tokens.

## Research Areas

Signature Schemes, Post-Quantum Cryptography, Secure Multi-party Computation
