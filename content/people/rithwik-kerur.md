Rithwik Kerur started his PhD at the University of California, Santa Barbara in 2024. He obtained a B.S. in Computer Science from the University of California, Irvine.
His research focuses on distributed systems, consensus, and information dispersal.
His LinkedIn profile is available at [linkedin.com/in/rithwikkerur](https://www.linkedin.com/in/rithwikkerur/).

From June to September 2026, he was a Research Intern at Cloudflare, where he worked on analyzing [Meerkat](https://blog.cloudflare.com/meerkat-introduction/)'s safety and optimizing log compaction.

## Research Areas

Distributed Systems, Consensus, Information Dispersal, Systems
