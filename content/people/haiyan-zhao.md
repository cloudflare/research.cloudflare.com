Haiyan Zhao is a PhD student in Computer Science at the New Jersey Institute of Technology. Her research focuses on understanding and interpreting representations in large language models, including activation inversion, natural-language explanations of internal model features, sparse autoencoders, and model steering. Her personal website is available at [hy-zhao23.github.io](https://hy-zhao23.github.io).

From May 2026 to August 2026, she was a Research Engineer Intern at Cloudflare, where she worked on SoftAgent and helped develop a new ranking algorithm.

## Research Areas

LLM Interpretability, Representation Understanding, Explainable AI, Actionable Explainability
