Philipp-Florens Lehwalder is a PhD student at the Chair of Applied Cryptography at the Technical University Darmstadt, Germany.
He obtained a M.Sc. in IT security and a B.Sc. in Computer Science from the Technical University Darmstadt.
His research focuses on privacy-preserving cryptography, especially anonymous credentials and zero-knowledge proofs.
His personal website is available at [lehwalder.net](https://lehwalder.net).

From June to September 2026, he was a Research Intern at Cloudflare, where he worked on zero-knowledge proofs over legacy credentials using Longfellow for Private Access Control Tokens (PACT).

## Research Areas

Cryptography, Anonymous Credentials, Zero-Knowledge Proofs, Privacy-Preserving Systems
